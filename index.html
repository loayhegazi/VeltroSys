Questions

What is at the heart of VeltroSys?

Managed IT is our flagship: AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) and managed services. Tell us about your environment to discuss the scope that fits your business.

Which other services do you offer?

Hosting includes VPS, Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace, cloud backup & DR. Infrastructure covers networks, cybersecurity, hardware, CCTV and VoIP. Development & Consulting covers consulting, ERP and custom apps.

Does the launch offer cover every service?

The launch discount is for the first 20 VPS Cloud users. Ask about the details and availability; it is not a portfolio-wide discount.
